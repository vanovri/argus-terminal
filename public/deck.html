Matching must be deterministic, order flow should be visible beyond top-of-book, and obviously bad orders should be rejected before they touch the book. Argus implements a simplified version of each.

SLIDE 04 // EXECUTION KERNEL WAVE 2 HIGH-FREQUENCY UPGRADE

PRICE-TIME PRIORITY (FIFO) vs. ZERO-ALLOC WASM SLAB ENGINE

`SlabOrderbook` (`src/engine/slabOrderbook.js`) replaces heap-allocated JS order objects with contiguous `Float64Array`/`Int32Array` arenas, intrusive doubly-linked FIFO queues, and an inline WebAssembly kernel.

1.48M ops/s SLAB ARENA MATCHING THROUGHPUT
MEMORY LAYOUTZERO-GC ARCHITECTURE

Contiguous TypedArray Slab + WASM Kernel

Every order slot is indexed `0..MAX_CAPACITY-1` in pre-allocated typed arrays (`prices`, `sizes`, `timestamps`, `nextIdx`, `prevIdx`) with a free-list stack and O(1) handle-based cancellation.

SLAB SLOT INVARIANT & FIFO PRIORITY Match(incoming, resting) iff:
• BUY: P_in ≥ P_ask[head]  |  SELL: P_in ≤ P_bid[head]
• Priority: (Price_A ≻ Price_B) ∨ (Price_A == Price_B ∧ Seq_A < Seq_B)
• Cancel(orderId): O(1) intrusive doubly-linked list unlink

SLIDE 05 // SIMULATED MARKET DATA PRICE MODEL & ORDER-FLOW METRICS

MEAN-REVERTING PRICES, VPIN, MICRO-PRICE & KYLE'S LAMBDA

A simple mean-reverting random walk generates the simulated prices; textbook order-flow metrics are then computed from the simulated book and trades. Useful for learning the formulas — not a calibrated market model.

10 BUCKETS VOLUME BUCKETS PER VPIN ESTIMATE
1. MEAN-REVERTING RANDOM WALK (OU-INSPIRED) dS_t ≈ θ(μ - S_t)dt + σ dW_t
Each 400 ms tick nudges the simulated price back toward a base price μ and adds a random shock. A simplified discrete step, not a fitted model.
2. KYLE'S LAMBDA (PRICE-IMPACT ESTIMATE) ΔP = λ · Q  →  λ ≈ Σ|ΔP·Q| / ΣQ²
Estimated from recent simulated trades (falls back to spread / top-of-book size), reported in bps per $100k notional.
3. VPIN (EASLEY–LÓPEZ DE PRADO–O'HARA) & MICRO-PRICE VPIN = (Σ_{\tau=1}^n |V_\tau^S - V_\tau^B|) / (n · V)  |  P_micro = (V_bid · P_ask + V_ask · P_bid) / (V_bid + V_ask)
Computed over n = 10 volume buckets of recent simulated trades; with too few trades it falls back to an imbalance-based proxy. Above 0.55 the quant briefing scales down its suggested position size.

QUOTING MATHAVELLANEDA & STOIKOV (2008)

Inventory-Adjusted Reservation Price

To limit inventory build-up, the market maker shifts its reservation price $r(s,q,t)$ away from mid-price $s$ in proportion to current inventory $q$, risk aversion $\gamma$, and variance $\sigma^2$:

AVELLANEDA-STOIKOV RESERVATION PRICE & OPTIMAL SPREAD r(s, q, t) = s - q · γ · σ² (T - t)
δ^a + δ^b = γ · σ² (T - t) + (2 / γ) · ln(1 + γ / κ)
